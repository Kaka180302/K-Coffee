## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Warm Minimalism** with tactile, **frosted glassmorphism** to evoke the atmosphere of an upscale, slow-pour specialty coffee bar. It strips away digital clutter in favor of generous cream-toned negative space, editorial typography, and delicate translucent surfaces.

The interface serves discerning coffee enthusiasts, home baristas, and specialty roastery patrons who value origin transparency, meticulous process craft, and sensory indulgence. Visuals balance grounded, earthy tones with luminous, feather-light glass panels that elevate coffee discovery into an intimate, curated ritual.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#2C1810` Espresso Dark):** Anchors high-priority actions, critical UI states, primary headers, and high-contrast structural accents. Supported by secondary roast shade `#4A2E1B` for hover fills and layered surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C48B57` Roasted Hazel):** Functions as the primary interactive accent, highlighting badges, purchase conversion pathways, active filters, and warm tactile details. Complemented by `#D4A373` (Gold Cream) for subtle borders and soft glow states.
- **Tertiary (`#263628` Forest Moss Green):** Evokes single-origin coffee plants, terroir notes, sustainable processing methods, and freshness cues. Paired with `#4A5B4C` (Sage) for neutral botanical badges and status indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F6F0` Warm Cream):** Sets the base background, with `#F3ECE2` (Sand) acting as the inset container and secondary layer tint.
- **Text & Reading:** Deep espresso `#23140C` for primary headings, `#4D3D35` for sustained editorial reading, and `#7E6F66` for metadata, captions, and inactive labels.
- **Glass Tinting:** Translucent white panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) anchored with fine light borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary craft of **Playfair Display** with the clean geometric balance of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display** handles brand storytelling, product titles, tasting notes headings, and featured editorial quotes. It brings warmth, humanity, and historic roastery craft to the experience.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** is used systematically across user interface elements, functional metadata (e.g., elevation, processing method, roast curves), pricing tiers, and actions.
- Use tabular numbering in Plus Jakarta Sans for prices, weights, and bag sizing chips to maintain alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt modular rhythm within a 12-column responsive fluid grid (max width 1320px). 

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (under 768px, 4 columns), Tablet (768px–1024px, 8 columns), Desktop (1024px+, 12 columns).
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop layout uses `margin: 3rem` with `gutter: 1.5rem`. Tablet condenses to `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Mobile scales to `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Rhythm Philosophy:** Emphasize generous vertical whitespace around glass panels to let the cream underlay breathe. Product listings, tasting card carousels, and brewing guides should never feel dense or crowded.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines frosted glass surfaces with diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadows, avoiding sterile grey drops.

- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Glass Containers):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `blur(12px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Menus, Sticky Cart Pill):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `blur(16px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`
  - Shadow: `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Coffee Origin Overlays):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `blur(20px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The design system uses a rounded geometry that feels organic and approachable, reminiscent of smoothed river stones and ceramic pour-over carafes. 

Standard components (product cards, input fields, dropdown menus) use standard corner radii (`rounded-lg`, 16px). Feature cards and showcase modals expand to soft envelopes (`rounded-xl`, 24px). Buttons, origin badges, roast tags, and utility pills use full pill geometry (`rounded-full`, 9999px) to establish a distinct, friendly tactile contrast against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Espresso Dark (`#2C1810`) background, cream text (`#F9F6F0`), pill shape, subtle hover translation (-1px vertical) transitioning to rich coffee (`#4A2E1B`) with warm amber focus rings (`#C48B57`).
  - *Secondary / Glass Button:* `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`, espresso text, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`. Hovers increase opacity to 90%.
  - *Tertiary / Text Button:* Roasted Hazel (`#C48B57`) text, underline offset with smooth opacity transitions.
- **Roast Level & Origin Badges:**
  - *Light Roast:* `#F3ECE2` background, `#7E6F66` text.
  - *Medium Roast:* `rgba(196, 139, 87, 0.15)` amber wash, `#8C5928` text.
  - *Dark Roast:* `#2C1810` dark espresso fill, `#F9F6F0` text.
  - *Terroir & Farm Badges:* Forest moss tint `rgba(38, 54, 40, 0.10)` with `#263628` text and leaf/origin icons.
- **Glass Product Cards:**
  - `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`, 20px padding, 18px rounded corners. Image container uses an inner `#F3ECE2` tint with an aspect ratio of 4:5. Hover triggers a subtle image zoom and activates a primary quick-add pill.
- **Input Fields & Dropdowns:**
  - Base surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.50)` with `1px solid rgba(196, 139, 87, 0.30)`, 12px corner radii. Focus shifts surface to crisp white, border to `#C48B57`, and introduces a 3px ring of `rgba(196, 139, 87, 0.15)`.
- **Flavor Profile & Tasting Notes Chips:**
  - Compact rounded-full pills, borderless `#F3ECE2` background, `#4D3D35` label typography, punctuated by 4px warm hazel indicator dots.
- **Fluting & Process Trackers (Coffee Specific):**
  - Segmented sliders tracking grind size (Turkish to French Press) and sensory balance (Acidity, Body, Sweetness) using thin `#D4A373` tracks and `#2C1810` circular grab handles.
